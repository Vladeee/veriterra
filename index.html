    Photo style · Basics

    Index

    Basic rules

    Photography shows real work in real fields. Until it exists, layouts use marked placeholders.

    1. Real growers and real crops. No stock, no AI renders.
    2. Daylight: early morning or late afternoon, no hard flash.
    3. Close on the work: hands, drip lines, roots, leaves.
    4. The crop is the hero; the bag plays a supporting part.
    5. Honest colour: soil looks like soil, no heavy filters.
    6. Calm backgrounds that leave room for type.
