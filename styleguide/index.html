Typography

Three roles: display for headlines, body for reading, mono for labels and data. The faces below are system placeholders until the Veriterra typefaces are chosen.

Typefaces

Display Aa Headlines and large numbers.
Body Aa Reading text and interface.
Mono Aa Labels, formulas, specs, data.

Scale

Large sizes are fluid: they grow with the screen between a minimum and a maximum. Sizes are measured live on this screen.

Rules

  • Sentence caseHeadlines are written like sentences. No all-caps headlines.
  • Numerals for numbers3,000 m², 25 kg, 4 stages. Facts are easier to scan than words.
  • Mono only for dataLabels, formulas and specs. Never for full sentences.
  • Short linesBody text stays within about 65 characters per line.
