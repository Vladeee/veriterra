Brand essence

Hypotheses, not approved. They become rules once the brief is signed off.

The idea: one loop

Each company feeds the next. That loop is what makes four young businesses read as one group.

  1. Land
  2. Product
  3. Person
  4. Space

Mission

To be written after the interview. It must say what changes for whom, in one sentence, with no adjectives doing the work.

Personality

Positions on each scale get set in the next interview round. Each company can sit at a different point; the holding sets the range.

  • QuietLoud
  • TechnicalSensory
  • LocalGlobal
  • RuggedRefined
  • SeriousPlayful

Tone of voice

  • Specific beats grandA number, a place or a fact instead of an adjective.
  • Say what existsName what is built, what is planned and when. Never blur the two.
  • Plain EnglishShort sentences, active verbs. Written for a reader whose first language may not be English.
We say3,000 m² of greenhouses on five acres in Koggala.
We don't sayA next-generation agro-park of the future.
We sayPicked in the morning, on the restaurant table by evening.
We don't sayHarmony of nature, comfort and mindful living.
We sayFertilizer formulated for the crop and its growth stage.
We don't sayInnovative solutions for a sustainable planet.
We sayPhase one is greenhouses. Processing starts once they sell.
We don't sayA unique holistic experience that stays in your heart.

Headings and body

Field note · Koggala

Food grown a short drive from the kitchen

Greenhouses near the coast supply hotel kitchens on the island every week.

Body copy is set at --text-md with a relaxed line height and a measure of about 65 characters. Paragraphs stay short. Numbers are written as numerals: 3,000 m², 5 acres, 20 units. Links use the brand accent and are always underlined.

A third-level heading

Use headings to make a page scannable, not to decorate it. One idea per paragraph.

08

Photography and illustration

Real or nothing. A visitor who spots one fake image stops trusting every real one.

Principles

  • Real or nothingNo AI-generated image is ever presented as a real place, product or person. Until a photo exists, show a hatched placeholder.
  • Show the workHands, soil, substrate, produce, tools, the site at different stages. People doing their actual jobs, not posing.
  • Honest scaleWide shots that show how big things really are. No hero angles that make five acres look like five hundred.
  • Label visualisationsArchitectural renders are allowed, always marked "Visualisation" in the corner.
  • Natural lightShoot in daylight, grade lightly. No orange sunset filter on everything.
  • Illustration explainsMaps, diagrams and charts in the icon style: line, one accent, real data. No decorative clip art.
