## 2. What is established

- **There will be four websites, one per company.** Whether the holding gets its own site is still open.
- **The holding has no name yet.** It is not a customer-facing brand, so for now it works only as an endorsement.
- **The visitors are potential customers**, not investors. Each site sells to a different buyer (see the table above). The exact target action for each site is still to be confirmed.
- **Content language: English.**
- **The existing presentations are context only.** Their colours, logos, typefaces and imagery are explicitly *not* a reference. New presentations will be made from this system.
- **The current imagery is AI-generated placeholder material.** None of it may be shipped as if it were real.
- **Real references, fonts and assets come later.**

## 3. Working hypotheses (proposed, waiting for approval)

1. **The loop is the idea.** Each company feeds the next: Veriterra feeds the soil, Verdo grows the food, Vaya serves it to guests and Viva builds the places. The existing decks summarise it as *Land → Product → Person → Space*. That loop is what makes four early-stage businesses read as one group rather than four small bets.
2. **One system, five themes.** Typefaces, grid, components and motion are shared. Each company gets its own accent palette. Technically this means one `tokens.css` with a `data-brand` theme per company.
3. **Endorsed architecture.** Each company leads on its own site. The holding appears as a quiet endorsement ("part of …"), not as the hero.

## 5. Non-negotiables already agreed

- **No generic AI slop:** no AI renders presented as reality, no "harmony of nature, comfort and mindful living" copy, no interchangeable eco-luxury clichés.
- **Specific beats grand:** a number, a place or a fact beats an adjective.
- **It must work on an iPad:** shared and presented from one.
